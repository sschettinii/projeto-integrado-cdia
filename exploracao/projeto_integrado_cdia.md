## Aprendizado contínuo (Lifelong Learning)

**Definição** (IBM): Continual learning is an artificial intelligence (AI) learning approach that involves sequentially training a model for new tasks while preserving previously learned tasks. Models incrementally learn from a continuous stream of nonstationary data, and the total number of tasks to be learned is not known in advance.
